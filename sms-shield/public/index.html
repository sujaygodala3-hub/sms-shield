      Rights and letters

      The rules, the process, the letter.

      Everything you need to dispute a medical bill, in order. Your letter fills itself in from Bill Check.

      Dispute procedure

      Five steps, in order

      1. 01

        Don't pay yet, and ask for an itemized bill

        Ask the billing office for an itemized bill with billing codes, and ask them to pause collections while you review it. Check for duplicate charges and services you didn't get.

      2. 02

        Compare it to your Explanation of Benefits

        Your insurer's EOB shows what you actually owe. If the bill asks for more than the EOB's "patient responsibility", that gap is what you dispute.

      3. 03

        Send a written dispute

        Send the letter below to the billing office and keep a copy. Name the rule that applies: No Surprises Act, 501(r) financial assistance, or your Good Faith Estimate.

      4. 04

        Appeal or apply

        Denied claim: file an internal appeal with your plan within 180 days of the denial notice. Nonprofit hospital: apply for financial assistance; they must accept applications for at least 240 days after the first post-discharge bill.

      5. 05

        Escalate

        Surprise billing: file a complaint with the No Surprises Help Desk at 1-800-985-3059. Uninsured and billed $400+ over your estimate: start patient-provider dispute resolution within 120 days of the bill. Denied appeal: request external review.
